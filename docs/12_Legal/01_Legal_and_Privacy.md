# Legal & Compliance Framework

## 1. Terms of Service (ToS)
**Key Clauses Required:**
- **Age Restriction:** Must be 13+ to use HabitFlow (COPPA compliance) and 16+ for EU users (GDPR).
- **No Medical Advice:** Explicit disclaimer that the AI Coach and Mood Tracking features are for personal productivity and self-reflection, NOT professional mental health or medical advice.
- **User Generated Content (UGC):** Users are responsible for what they post in Squads. We reserve the right to ban users for inappropriate behavior (hate speech, spam).
- **Virtual Currency:** HabitFlow "Coins" have no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged for fiat currency. We reserve the right to modify the gamification economy at any time.

## 2. Privacy Policy
**Data Collection & Usage:**
- **Local Data:** Emphasize that the majority of data lives on the user's device (IndexedDB) and is not accessible by HabitFlow staff unless synced.
- **Cloud Sync:** Clearly state that data synced to Supabase is stored securely and only used to provide cross-device functionality and social Squad features.
- **Third-Party Services:** 
  - AI Providers (e.g., OpenAI/Gemini via our API): We do NOT use user data to train our foundational AI models. Prompts sent to the AI are ephemeral.
  - Analytics (e.g., Vercel/PostHog): Anonymized telemetry for app performance.

## 3. Licensing
- **Codebase:** HabitFlow proprietary software. Open-sourcing certain UI components (e.g., Glassmorphism library) is planned for the future under MIT license.
- **Assets:** Any 3D assets or icons used must comply with their respective commercial licenses (e.g., Lucide React is ISC).
